The History: Why I Made Volyap

Volyap comes from my deepest desire an ordinary student with a heart for extraordinary impact. This brand is my way to speak up and invite everyone to join the "Volyap For Palestine" campaign because I believe this movement can keep reminding the world of the injustice that’s happening. And that silence is never neutral. Some of us may not all be able to go there but through the sweater we wear, we can carry the message of freedom anywhere.

What does “Volyap” mean?

VOLYAP is an abbreviation of Volya Yafuz Pian, Volya is Slavic for "will", Yafuz is Arabic for "win", Pian is Finnish for "soon". And for the logo is an illustration of an olive fruit which is one of the characteristics of Palestine.

Vision and Mission

Volyap has a motto "Victory is near" and 70% of profits from Volyap sweater sales will be donated for our brothers and sisters in Palestine. We use a pre-order system, so you only pay when your order is ready. After the specified period ends, the donation results will be shared transparently through Volyap's social media accounts.
